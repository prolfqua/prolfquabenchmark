# prolfquabenchmark 0.3.4

- Four ProteoBench datasets (two conditions, three runs each): the DIA single-cell module (PXD049412, Orbitrap Astral, human/yeast) as DIA-NN 2.5.0 (`SingleCell_DIANN`) and MaxQuant 2.7.5.0 DIA evidence.txt (`SingleCell_MaxQuant`), and the diaPASEF module (human/yeast/E. coli) as DIA-NN 2.5.0 (`diaPASEF_DIANN`) and Spectronaut 21.0 (`diaPASEF_Spectronaut`). Each is one ProteoBench submission without match-between-runs, downloaded from the ProteoBench server into `results/downloads/proteobench` and checked by MD5, with the species of each protein group from the ProteoBench FASTA. They are read with prolfquapp's readers and carry the identification confidence these give (DIA-NN and MaxQuant 1 - PEP, Spectronaut 1 - q-value), which varies across samples. True positives: yeast in the single-cell module; yeast and E. coli in diaPASEF, where yeast goes down from A to B and E. coli up.
- New dataset `IonStar_MetaMorpheus`: IonStar reanalysed with MetaMorpheus 1.0.7 and FlashLFQ 1.3 with match-between-runs (PXD057758), the `QuantifiedPeaks.tsv` of the APB example datasets on Zenodo (record 24418695), downloaded and checked by MD5. Match-between-runs peaks carry the identification confidence 1 - PIP PEP, which is below 0.5 for a fifth of the observations, MS/MS peaks 1.
- The ground truth of a dataset can name positives expected to decrease (`positive: {..., down: <pattern>}`); `annotate_ground_truth()` negates their fold change and t statistic, so the scores, which rank by signed statistics, rank them like the positives that increase.
- The MaxQuant datasets (IonStar, CPTAC) hand prolfqua the identification confidence 1 - PEP, which prolfqua 1.9.11 uses as model weight, as prolfquapp's MaxQuant reader does; they set `ident_q_value`, which prolfqua 1.9.11 removed. The step timings still run with earlier prolfqua releases.
- `prolfqua_ropeca_impute_nested` and `prolfqua_ropecaV2_impute_nested` are out again: they ranked last or next to last on all three peptide datasets, and prolfqua 1.9.10 removed them. Their 1.9.9 scores stay in `score_history.tsv`; `Score_history` shows only the models of the latest release. prolfquabenchmark now requires prolfqua 1.9.10.
- New benchmark methods `prolfqua_rlm`, `prolfqua_ropeca_impute_nested` and `prolfqua_ropecaV2_impute_nested` (ROPECA on peptide models refit at the limit of detection, prolfqua 1.9.9); prolfquabenchmark now requires prolfqua 1.9.9.
- `Benchmark_results` plots pAUC_5 against the number of contrasts computed (protein and contrast pairs with a result, all contrasts together) instead of the proteins tested averaged over the contrasts; the Pareto front is the same. The same-pairs panels and `benchmark_same_pairs.tsv` give the front method's contrasts computed (column `contrasts`).
- Same pairs: a method may be on the Pareto front because it tests fewer, easier proteins. The new pipeline step `score_same_pairs` scores every method that tests all the pairs of a front method's protein and contrast pairs on exactly these pairs (`inst/extdata/benchmark_same_pairs.tsv`), and `Benchmark_results` shows them per dataset in a figure after the Pareto front: one panel per front method's set of contrasts, each with its own x axis, the methods scored on these contrasts on a dashed line. New exported functions `same_pairs_scores()`, `score_on_pairs()` and `pareto_optimal()`; the pairs come from prolfqua's `ContrastsComparison`, so prolfquabenchmark now requires prolfqua 1.9.8.
- Benchmark rerun with prolfqua 1.9.7 and its scores added to the score history. prolfqua 1.9.6 computes each protein's limma contrasts from its own observed samples: `prolfqua_limma`, `prolfqua_deqms_voom`, `prolfqua_limpa` and `prolfqua_limpa_nested` score higher (pAUC_5 on IonStar MaxQuant: limma 63.9 to 69.2, deqms_voom 57.9 to 64.5, limpa 60.0 to 67.0), and the vooma methods test more proteins; the other methods are unchanged.
- The documentation site is built with altdoc (`make site`, GitHub workflow `altdoc.yml`) instead of pkgdown; the vignettes are at `https://prolfqua.github.io/prolfquabenchmark/vignettes/<name>.html` (formerly `articles/`). The vignettes read the benchmark tables of the installed package, so run `make install` after `make benchmark` before rendering them.
- `Benchmark_results` lists the methods once each (16 rows instead of one per dataset and method), with the number of datasets they ran on; the per-dataset rows and fit times are in the linked `benchmark_methods.tsv`.
- New `method_scales()`: ggplot2 colour, shape and line type scales by method, from prolfqua's `facade_styles.tsv` (colour of the facade's family in the prolfqua factory figure, shape and line type per facade) plus dark grey open shapes for MSstats, proDA and msqrob2. Both vignettes style every method plot with it.
- The benchmarks run in a Snakemake pipeline (`workflow/Snakefile`, `config/config.yaml`; `make benchmark`) instead of in the vignettes. Each dataset is one input file (IonStar MaxQuant peptides.txt and proteinGroups.txt, IonStar FragPipe MSstats.csv and combined_protein.tsv, CPTAC study 6 peptides.txt) and is prepared once, every method (prolfqua facades, MSstats, proDA, msqrob2) is fitted to every dataset that provides its input (peptides, proteins, the search software's protein table or MSstats' feature table; the external tools take only their own input, never the proteins prolfqua aggregated) and scored, and the results are exported to `inst/extdata`. Snakemake comes from a uv environment (`pyproject.toml`).
- New vignette `Benchmark_results`: per dataset its preprocessing steps (from `config/config.yaml`, exported as `benchmark_preprocessing.tsv`), the scores of every method with the number of contrasts it computed, ROC curves up to a false positive rate of 0.2, the partial AUC up to a false positive rate of 0.05 (`pAUC_5`, new column of `benchmark_scores.tsv`) against the number of proteins tested (as in ProteoBench, first in each section; points labelled with the method, ggrepel in Suggests; the Pareto front drawn) and the false discovery proportion against the nominal FDR. Every figure and table has a caption naming the metric, the axes, the encoding and the changed and unchanged proteins (new columns `positive` and `negative` of `benchmark_datasets.tsv`). Colour marks the model family of the prolfqua factory figure (linear models, limma, limpa, LOD imputation, peptide level, hurdle), point shape and line type the model within its family, and MSstats, proDA and msqrob2 are dark grey with open shapes; a method looks the same in every plot of both vignettes. The analysis vignettes `Benchmark_prolfqua`, `Benchmark_MSStats`, `Benchmark_proDA`, `Benchmark_msqrob2`, `Benchmark_hurdle` and `Benchmark_ropeca` and the studies `Benchmark_null_split`, `Benchmark_pipeline_demo`, `Benchmark_Model_IonStar_With2Factors` and `SqueezeVar_comparison` are removed.
- New vignette `Score_history` with the partial AUC (pAUC_10, all contrasts pooled) and step timings of each recorded prolfqua release: per dataset a captioned figure of pAUC_10 per release and model, then a table of each model's latest pAUC_10 with the change from its previous release (MSstats, proDA and msqrob2 at their latest run only), and figures of the elapsed time of the lm, limma, lm_impute and rlm facades and of transform, aggregate, lm fit and contrasts. Every other score, also per contrast, is in the linked `inst/extdata/score_history.tsv`. `make score-history` appends the pipeline's scores and timings for the installed prolfqua (`REPLACE=1` reruns a recorded version). The history starts with prolfqua 1.9.0, rerun with the pipeline: `make score-history-release V=1.9.0 PROLFQUA=f4dfb7f3 PROLFQUAPP=18914707` installs that release and the prolfquapp of its time into a library of their own (`inst/scripts/install_release.sh`), fits every prolfqua method whose facade the release has (all except `ropecaV2_nested`, 42 fits on the five datasets) and records the rows with the date of the prolfqua commit. Each tagged prolfqua release adds its block.
- The hurdle model is benchmarked as prolfqua's `hurdle_nested` facade (`prolfqua_hurdle_nested`); the hand-built variants `hurdle_binomial`, `hurdle_binomial_firth_unbound` and `hurdle_binomial_firth_df4` are dropped (Firth against plain binomial detection and msqrob2's combination rule changed the scores by less than 0.2 points; without the dispersion bound the ranking was worse). New method `msqrob2_protein`: msqrob2 without its detection model, fitted to the search software's protein table (MaxQuant LFQ intensities, FragPipe combined_protein.tsv).
- prolfqua models are named `prolfqua_<facade>`: `prolfqua_lm`, `prolfqua_lm_impute`, `prolfqua_limma`, `prolfqua_deqms`, `prolfqua_deqms_voom` and `prolfqua_limpa` on proteins, `prolfqua_lmer_nested`, `prolfqua_ropeca_nested`, `prolfqua_ropecaV2_nested`, `prolfqua_limpa_nested` and `prolfqua_hurdle_nested` on peptides; DEqMS and limpa are new. The former `prolfqua_lm` (`lm_missing`, now in prolfquatakeoutmissing) is dropped from the benchmark and the score history; prolfquabenchmark no longer uses prolfquatakeoutmissing.
- Each dataset and method has one name: the MaxQuant results formerly under `IonStar_MQ`, `IonStar_MQ_LFQ` and `IonStar_MQ_medpolish` are `IonStar_MaxQuant` (peptides.txt), `IonStar_MaxQuant_protein` (proteinGroups.txt LFQ intensities; proDA runs here only, proDA on the proteins prolfqua aggregated is dropped); MSstats on MaxQuant's evidence.txt, the only method on that file, is dropped, and the duplicate `limma` of the ROPECA benchmark is dropped (same model as `prolfqua_limma`).
- CPTAC is benchmarked again; its MaxQuant peptides come from Bioconductor `MsDataHub` (`cptac_a_b_peptides.txt()`, the file `msdata::quant()` provided before its deprecation).
- MSstats on FragPipe's MSstats.csv gets proteins with at least 2 peptides, like the other methods (it tested 1152 single-peptide proteins more), and its infinite fold changes (one condition without observations, no test) are no longer ranked.
- proDA results are reproducible: its fit draws random numbers, so it is now seeded; two unseeded fits of the same data differed by up to 1.3 in log fold change. msqrob2 results carry each protein's average intensity instead of one value per contrast, proDA results their average abundance, and CPTAC uses `_YEAST` as the negative pattern for every tool.
- Uses no prolfqua function that prolfqua 1.9.5 deprecates: the msqrob2 and hurdle vignettes and `tolong()` convert result tables with `tibble::as_tibble(rownames = )` instead of `prolfqua::matrix_to_tibble()`, and the paper supplement applies ROPECA with `ContrastsROPECA` and shows contrast weights with `Contrasts$get_linfct()`.
- The vignettes and the squeezeVar benchmark script no longer set an isotope label, which label-free prolfqua 1.9.5 removed.
- Requires prolfqua >= 1.9.5; the vignettes call its renamed functions and simulator arguments (`lfqdata_to_summarized_experiment()`, `n_proteins`).
- New vignette `Benchmark_null_split`: splits the runs of each IonStar dilution at random into two pseudo-groups and tests them against each other with `lm`, `limma` and `lm_impute` after no normalization, `robscale()` or human-subset `robscale_subset()`. It reports the share of p < 0.05 and p < 0.01, FDR hits, KS distance and p-value histograms over 20 splits, plus a split along the two run blocks. Both normalizations are calibrated; without normalization single splits are far off; the run-block split gives about 100 FDR hits per 1000 proteins.

- New vignette `Benchmark_ropeca` comparing prolfqua's `ropeca_nested` with the new `ropecaV2_nested`, `lmer_nested` and `limma`: on simulated proteins with correlated peptides and on the unchanged human proteins of IonStar it tabulates the share of p < 0.05 and p < 0.01 by number of peptides, and on IonStar it scores the E. coli vs human ranking (AUC, partial AUC, FDR vs FDP). Requires prolfqua >= 1.9.4.
- `Benchmark_hurdle` drops `firth_nested`, which prolfqua 1.9.3 removed, and its hurdle variants; the msqrob2-rule variant now combines `lm_impute` with `binomial_firth_nested` (`hurdle_binomial_firth_df4`). The calibration text keeps the `firth_nested` result: the share of unchanged proteins with detection p < 0.05 rose from 1.3% to 7.7% with the number of peptides.
- New vignette `Benchmark_hurdle` benchmarking prolfqua's hurdle contrasts (`ContrastsHurdleFacade`: `lm_impute` combined with `firth_nested`, `binomial_nested` or `binomial_firth_nested`) against `lm_impute` alone and msqrob2's hurdle model on the IonStar MaxQuant peptides. It also scores msqrob2's rule of combining the detection p-value for fully detected proteins too, and tabulates how often each detection model calls the unchanged human proteins significant, by number of peptides.
- The example datasets `data_benchmarkExample` and `data_test_confusion_matrix_scores` now ship with prolfquabenchmark (they were removed from prolfqua 1.9.0); load them with `prolfqua::prolfqua_data(<name>, package = "prolfquabenchmark")`.
- `SqueezeVar_comparison` no longer lists `lm_missing` among the prolfqua facades that feed variance moderation.

- New vignette `SqueezeVar_comparison` comparing variance moderation across `limma::squeezeVar()`, `msqrob2`, and
  `prolfqua::squeezeVarRob()`. It shows msqrob2 delegates to limma, and that `prolfqua::squeezeVarRob()` is limma's
  classic `fitFDist` plus a `min_df` filter — explaining why old MSqRob forked it (classic limma collapses to
  `df.prior = Inf` on fractional/low-df features). It documents that `prolfqua` reaches this regime through its `rlm` /
  `rfit`, `lmer_nested`, and imputation facades (fractional / low residual df; `firth` is not a consumer), and includes
  a benchmark (synthetic + real IonStar spike-in data) showing the former fork and modern `limma::squeezeVar()` give
  near-identical results — the evidence behind prolfqua migrating its moderation to `limma::squeezeVar()`.
- Began tracking user-visible changes in `NEWS.md`. For changes before this version, see the git history.
